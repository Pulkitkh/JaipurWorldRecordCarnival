Chapter Two · Why we gather

We are not an events company.
We are a gathering.

Jaipur World Record Carnival exists to give people a reason — and a professional, properly documented structure — to do something extraordinary together.

Our story

It began with a simple,
slightly stubborn idea.

That a world record is not a trophy for one person. It is the largest excuse ever invented for a community to work together.

Manmohan Agrawal set his first record and noticed something nobody talks about: the certificate mattered far less than the six months of people learning to coordinate, trust each other and hold a plan. So the work changed. Instead of chasing records, JWRC began building the gatherings that produce them — and treating the record itself as the receipt.

Seven Guinness World Records later, across six certifying bodies, the method has not changed. Find the people. Do the paperwork properly. Make the day unforgettable. Leave something behind that outlasts the photograph.

Our vision

A society where extraordinary things are achieved together, not alone.

We want every individual, institution and community in India to have a real opportunity to help create history — while strengthening the culture and the relationships that hold them together.

Our mission

To inspire people to dream bigger, collaborate stronger and achieve together.

Through professionally organised record initiatives, cultural celebrations and community-driven events that promote learning, leadership, unity, creativity and social responsibility.

What we hold to Eight values

Values are only real
when they cost you
something.

Each of these has, at some point, made us turn down an attempt. That is how we know they are load-bearing rather than decorative.

Unity

Great achievements happen when people work together, not when one person wins.

Community

Every individual matters. Every contribution matters. Including the smallest one.

Heritage

The craft that surrounds us is not decoration. The people who keep it alive get paid to teach it.

Innovation

New thinking, new categories, new formats — without discarding what came before.

Integrity

Transparency, honesty and professionalism. We would rather lose the booking.

Excellence

Every initiative should leave a mark that is still visible a year later.

Inclusion

Every age, background, ability and profession. No auditions, no entry fee.

Inspiration

Every event should make somebody watching think: I could do that too.

The beginning

One record, one realisation

A first successful attempt makes it obvious that the interesting part was never the number — it was the months of people learning to work as one.

Building the method

Schools come first

Educational institutions turn out to be the natural home for this: a ready community, a real appetite, and students who rise to responsibility instantly.

Widening

Causes ride along

NGOs and government departments discover that a record attempt earns the attention their campaigns deserve but rarely get.

Seven Guinness records

Credibility, then scale

Recognition across Guinness, Limca, Asia Book, India Book, Golden Book and Unique Book of Records — six independent bodies, one standard of evidence.

Now

A carnival, not a company

The work today is less about setting records and more about running the platform that lets anyone in Rajasthan propose one.

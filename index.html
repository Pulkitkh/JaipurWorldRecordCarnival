01 — Welcome Rajasthan

A state that has always
known how to gather.

Fairs that fill a desert. Processions that close a city. A wedding street where nobody remembers who was invited and nobody was turned away.

Rajasthan has been assembling enormous numbers of people, joyfully and without fuss, for centuries. The Jaipur World Record Carnival simply points that instinct at something the world happens to count.

Read our story
02 — Who we are

We are not an events
company. We are
a gathering.

JWRC plans, organises and documents large-scale record attempts — and treats every one of them as a reason for a community to meet.

We work with schools, colleges, universities, NGOs, government bodies, corporates, cultural groups, volunteers and individuals who simply turn up. We handle feasibility, paperwork, logistics and evidence. You bring the people.

Seven-time Guinness World Record holder

Manmohan Agrawal

Founder · Jaipur World Record Carnival

A builder by profession and a convenor by instinct. Secretary of the Rajasthan Affordable Housing Development Association and a member of the RERA Conciliation Forum, he has spent years proving that the hardest part of a world record is never the record — it is getting ten thousand people to believe it is possible.

03 — Why records matter

A record is only ever the excuse. What we are really building is the reason ten thousand people had to stand in the same place, on the same morning, for each other.

01

It teaches

A live lesson in planning, discipline, teamwork and leadership that no classroom can stage.

02

It unites

Age, income, language and background stop mattering the moment everyone counts to the same number.

03

It carries a cause

Cleanliness, literacy, health, water, tree cover — a record puts a message where a pamphlet never reaches.

04

It outlives itself

Long after the certificate is framed, participants still say “I was there.” That is the actual record.

04 — Our journey Idea → History

How an idea becomes
a world record.

Six stages, and we are beside you for all of them. Most people arrive with nothing more than a thought and a rough sense of how many friends they can call.

1

The Spark

You bring an idea. We check whether a category exists — or whether we build a new one.

2

The Blueprint

Rules, evidence standards, feasibility, budget, and a date that actually works.

3

The Gathering

Institutions, volunteers, partners and participants — mobilised and briefed.

4

The Rehearsal

Venue, safety, stewarding, timing, counting method. Nothing left to the day.

5

The Moment

Execution, independent witnesses, photography, videography, evidence capture.

6

The Legacy

Adjudication, certification, the story — and the community that stays behind.

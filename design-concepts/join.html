Four ways in

Participate

Come to the next attempt. Bring your family, your class, your street, your whole colony. There is no fee, no audition and no prior experience of anything. You stand where you are told, you do the thing, and afterwards you are part of it.

Anyone · any age · no experience needed

Volunteer

The crew behind an attempt is where the real education happens. Stewarding, counting, briefing, registration, documentation, crowd flow, first aid liaison. You will be trained, given genuine responsibility, and credited by name.

Students · professionals · retired · anyone with a free Sunday
Join the crew

Partner

Bring your school, college, university, NGO, department or company into an attempt — as the host, as a participating body, or as a supporter. We start with an honest feasibility conversation, not a proposal document.

Schools · colleges · NGOs · government · corporates
